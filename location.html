Memorable Mykonos Villa Holidays

When in Mykonos, follow the flow, adopt the cosmopolitan chic or laid back lifestyle. Dine around at some of the finest gourmet restaurants of the Cyclades or in the privacy of your villa, dive in the sparkling blue waters of your pool or mingle with the jet-setters, the VIP’s and vacationing families at Elia beach. Celebrate life with friends in the intimate setting of your villa or rejoice your Mykonos villa holidays with legendary bar strolls. Be spotted or unseen, loud or silent as this is the island of contradictions and you can find all it is you seek in the reach of your palm while enjoying your dream holidays in Mykonos.

Things to do in mykonos.

Enjoy your morning coffee at Little Venice and take a picture at the famous windmills. Walk the cobblestone streets of Chora through the art galleries and designer boutiques and make a stop at the Church of Paraportiani. Meet Peter the Pelican and start your evening with a cocktail at some of the most renowned bars in town, dance the night away or salute the night with a glass of champagne in the soothing environs of your villa. Visit some of the best attractions in Mykonos and then head to discover the sacred island of Delos.
