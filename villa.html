The Villa

Exclusive Sea View Mykonos Villa Accommodation

Spread over 3 levels of luxurious spaces in Elia, Mykonos, this exclusive, sea view Mykonos villa with private pool emanates a feeling of bliss and relaxation in the most enjoyable of atmospheres. Beautifully appointed with modern comforts and charmingly adorned with sleek contemporary furniture, this enchanting villa in Mykonos welcomes you to surrender to all pleasures of luxury.

Gracious Private Villa in Mykonos

On the upper level of the villa you will find the cosy living room, one bathroom and a fully equipped kitchen where you can prepare a great variety of epicurean meals. Over the top, there is a stylish boat-like bedroom with a king size bed and a private bathroom where you can find your peace and quiet while taking in the miraculous vistas to the sea and sky. The ground floor features a master bedroom with a king size bed and a private bathroom while the lower floor boasts a second living room area, 2 bedrooms with twin beds, 1 bedroom with a king size bed and 3 bathrooms of polished cement with smooth curves and earthly colours. From all rooms on the inside of the villa the view is breath-taking and so is the view outdoors, uninterrupted… bedazzling.

Remarkable Mykonos Villa with Private Pool

Moving to the outdoor area of this sea view villa, you will be dazzled by the radiance of the floating water all around. Enjoy a refreshing swim in the scintillating waters of the infinity pool and bask in the sun on the cosy loungers. As the day unfolds enjoy a barbeque at the built-in table under the shaded pergola or a romantic dinner under the veil of stars until the time comes to salute the night with a cocktail at the bar by the pool at one of the finest villas for 10 persons in Mykonos, Greece.
